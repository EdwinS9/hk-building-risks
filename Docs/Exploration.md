# Data Exploration

## Age Distribution: DS1 vs DS2

![[age_distribution.png]]

Buildings that have received statutory inspection or repair notices (DS2) are substantially older than the general building stock (DS1). The DS1 distribution peaks around 35–45 years, reflecting the bulk of Hong Kong's post-war residential construction through the 1970s–90s. DS2's distribution is shifted roughly 12 years older (median 50.6 vs 37.8 years), with a pronounced concentration between 45–60 years — the cohort built in the 1960s–70s that now falls squarely within the Mandatory Building Inspection Scheme's enforcement window for buildings aged 30 years or more. This confirms that age is a strong signal for inspection risk: buildings in the 45–65 year range are disproportionately represented among those already under statutory notice.
